Sailors, Coconuts & the Monkey

Find the smallest initial pile of coconuts such that each of N sailors, one at a time overnight, splits the pile into N parts with one left over (given to the monkey) and hides their share — and in the morning the remaining pile divides evenly by N.

—
Closed form: odd N → N N − (N−1); even N → (N−1)·(N N − 1).
Runs entirely in your browser. No network calls.
